Real memecoin stories

When memes become markets.

Only verified, historical examples — used here to illustrate how the market moves, not to promise it will move the same way again.

2013 — PRESENT

Dogecoin

Started as a joke based on the "Doge" meme, built with no roadmap or serious utility. Its community grew around humor and generosity rather than technology.

Attention around Dogecoin has spiked repeatedly over the years, often tied to celebrity mentions and broader social-media cycles, driving sharp swings in its market value in both directions.

What beginners can learn: a strong, active community can sustain a project's attention for years, but attention-driven demand can reverse just as quickly as it arrives.

2020 — PRESENT

Shiba Inu

Launched as a self-described "Dogecoin killer," Shiba Inu built an ecosystem around its token and an active, highly online community.

It saw an extraordinary rise in attention and trading activity during 2021, followed by significant drawdowns typical of highly speculative assets.

What beginners can learn: rapid growth in attention doesn't equal fundamental value, and even large, well-known projects carry substantial volatility risk.

Past performance is not a guarantee of future results. These examples demonstrate how quickly markets can move — in both directions.
